Stairs that look custom-built

Stair capping covers your existing steps with new, durable materials — no need to rebuild the whole staircase. Every tread is measured and cut individually for a tight, precise fit, and we match the color to your new or existing floors.

  • Carpet & old material removal
  • Measured, custom-cut treads
  • Painted or matching risers
  • Stair nosing & landing transitions
  • Matched to vinyl, laminate or hardwood
  • Trim, caulk & clean finish
